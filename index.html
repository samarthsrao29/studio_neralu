WHO WE ARE.

At Studio Neralu, we believe in designing spaces that embody the spirit of ‘Neralu’, which in the native tongue literally means shadow. But within the framework of architecture it can mean so much more. From time immemorial man has always sought shade within the natural world. Shade as a means of shelter from all that the sun, the wind and the rain could manifest i.e., the natural world as we know it. Nature could be both a virtue and a vice at the same time. Thus designing spaces embodying the spirit of ‘Neralu’ both as a place of refuge and well-being is what we do at Studio Neralu.
